## Verification notes

- Surge formats and per-OS availability are listed on the official downloads page.
- TAL product pages show CLAP/VST3/AU and separate platform archives. Windows is listed here as CLAP/VST3 and macOS as CLAP/VST3/AU; their Linux archive is labeled VST2/VST3, with no Linux CLAP/AU claim.
- TDR product pages publish a format/OS table. AU is macOS-only; Nova's official product record additionally lists Linux x86-64 and VST3. Kotelnikov and Molotok are listed for Windows/macOS. These are standard/free editions, not paid GE editions.
- ChowDSP's official product page names BYOD's desktop formats and operating systems. The ChowMatrix manual specifies VST/VST3 and AU, and Windows, Linux, and Mac. This list makes no CLAP claim for ChowMatrix.
- MT Power's official site says free and AU/VST; AU is shown for macOS only, while Windows is listed as VST with its variant unspecified. The page does not verify VST3 on either OS.
- Dragonfly's official project page states Windows/macOS/Linux. Its official source project build/release metadata lists VST3 and CLAP without per-OS pairing; no AU claim is made.
- MeldaProduction's product pages mark each listed effect free and link it through MFreeFXBundle. The official [bundle feature page](https://develop.meldaproduction.com/MFreeFXBundle/features) supports VST3 on 64-bit Windows and VST3/AU on 64-bit macOS. The bundle offers optional paid upgrades; this does not make its paid features/products free. No CLAP or Linux support is claimed here.
